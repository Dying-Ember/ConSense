## OER reduction requirements

- Make available native paragraphs readable and usable before optional visual/table refinement finishes.
- Do not render/PNG-encode a page solely to discover that OCR is disabled or unavailable.
- Retain explicit missing-text/decoding/layout states. A scan without usable text still needs OCR and cannot be reported as parsed text.
- Keep the existing rich/template/canonical contracts and source/version/exclusion validation intact. Introduce the smallest explicit ordinary-reading seam where needed.
- Avoid duplicate full native/glyph/operator inventories in ordinary persistence and bound transient work by page/batch where possible.
- Reuse saved observations, existing embedding windows and unchanged vectors for availability; measure fresh complete-file processing separately from reuse.

## Product SL requirements

- Versions are a built-in reference-library feature, not an isolated test corpus.
