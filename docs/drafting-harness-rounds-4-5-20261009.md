The Sections primary answer puts designation only in optional `id`, leaving required `designation` absent. Its repair supplies the right rows but deletes an intervening sentence from the quote, so the citation is not contiguous. Both rejections remain observable. Neither fields nor citations are synthesized from the oracle.
